# Legal Department

The Legal module has four primary pages: Dashboard, Clients, Upload Clients, and Client Files. Clients referred from Collections are selected from existing, non-archived collection cases with `LEGAL` status. These records are not copied; client profiles and documents remain linked to the original `CollectionCustomer`.

Excel uploads accept XLSX, XLS, and CSV files. The Legal client import supports the workbook columns `#`, `Branches`, `ID Number`, `Installment Unique ID`, `Cust Full Name`, `serial`, `تاريخ المحضر`, `رقم المحضر`, `النيابة`, `تاريخ الحكم`, `الحكم`, and `Agency`, and retains the original values in each client's imported-file records. `Cust Full Name` maps to the client name, `ID Number` maps to National ID, and `Installment Unique ID` is retained as the external identifier. National ID is optional when an installment identifier is present. Excel date cells are read as dates; text dates remain unchanged to avoid changing their meaning.

The uploader must explicitly select an active MIS organization. `Branches` and `Agency` are preserved as source data and are never used to infer that organization. Preview shows distinct valid, invalid, and duplicate row counts with row-specific validation errors. Existing-client matching and duplicate checks use the selected organization's scope and available identifiers. Valid rows are saved in one database transaction while invalid or duplicate rows are reported without replacing existing customer data. Imported workbook records are appended to client history and appear in Client Files.

Legal client documents are stored through the existing MIS file-storage service and are linked to the customer record. PDF, JPEG, and PNG content is validated; uploads are limited to 10 MB. Legal access controls reads, while the existing Legal management permission controls imports, notes, and document changes.

The additive `AddLegalClientManagement` and `AddLegalImportSourceData` migrations create legal assignment, document metadata, and imported-source-data fields. They do not modify or remove collection customers, cases, or existing attachments. Apply the migrations through the normal deployment process before using client imports or document uploads.
